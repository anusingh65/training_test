Under the Hood

Architecture & AI Models

AI Model 1 — Anomaly Detection
Isolation Forest

Unsupervised ML. Trains on normal supply chain data only. No labeled counterfeit examples needed — isolates anomalies by how few random splits it takes to separate them.

Autoencoder

Neural network trained to reconstruct normal transactions. When reconstruction error is high, the transaction doesn’t match learned patterns → anomaly.

6 Domain Rules

AI Model 2 — Predictive Heatmap
DBSCAN Clustering

Groups flagged scans by geographic proximity (~5km radius). Identifies emerging hotspot clusters in real-time.

Time Decay

7-day exponential half-life. A 2-week-old flag counts for 25% intensity. Heatmap always reflects current threat landscape.

Trend Prediction

Linear regression on weekly cluster snapshots. Extrapolates 2 weeks ahead. Alerts if any cluster is predicted to double in size.

What Makes Us Different

Innovation + Impact

Every team here will build QR + blockchain verification. Here’s what we built on top of that.

Semi-Supervised Learning

Our AI trains on normal behavior only. No labeled counterfeit data needed — which doesn’t exist anyway. Catches anomalies nobody programmed it to look for. Deployable from day one.

Predictive, Not Reactive

Our heatmap doesn’t show where counterfeits were found. It predicts where they’ll appear 2 weeks from now. DBSCAN clustering + linear regression on weekly snapshots = proactive enforcement.

Self-Improving System

Government raids → confirmed seizure locations → ground truth training data → model retrains → better predictions. Closed feedback loop. Gets smarter with every enforcement action.

Patient Safety

Counterfeit antibiotics drive antimicrobial resistance — 700K+ deaths/year from AMR globally. MediChain gives every patient instant verification before they take a single pill.

Economic Justice

Rural pharmacies bear the blame for counterfeits they unknowingly stock. Real-time verification protects honest businesses and keeps local economies alive.

Public Health Intelligence

Every scan adds to India’s first real-time pharmaceutical quality map. Outbreaks caught in days, not months. Government gets data, not quarterly reports.

Trust in Healthcare

When patients doubt their medicine, they stop taking it. Verified drugs restore faith in the healthcare system — especially in underserved rural communities.

How We Scale

Verification fee per scan. India dispenses 30B drug units/year. Even 0.1% adoption = 30M scans/year.

95% smartphone penetration in urban India. UPI payments made QR scanning second nature.
